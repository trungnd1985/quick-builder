Every block can have a border, with a width, a line style, a colour from the site's colours, rounded corners and, except on Sections and Containers, space between the line and its content. Sites gain five corner radii (None, Small, Medium, Large and Full), which Designers edit under Design with the other tokens and which travel with site exports. Existing pages look exactly as before.
